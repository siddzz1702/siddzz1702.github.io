Three dashboard projects focused on operational performance, profitability and business-process visibility. Each project starts with a business question and uses data modelling, measures and visual reporting to make the analysis easier to explore.

Completed01 · Power BI

Customer Service Performance & Call Centre Analytics

Business problem: Managers need a consolidated view of call activity, waiting times, representative workload and branch performance to understand where service operations may need attention.

  • Prepared and analysed 559 records across 14 fields using Power Query.
  • Built DAX measures for total calls, incoming/outgoing activity, average incoming wait time, lost calls and abandoned calls.
  • Created report views for service overview, representative performance and North/South branch comparisons, including Top-10 and time-block analysis.

Completed02 · Power BI

Retail Profitability Analysis – Is Least Profitable Always Unprofitable?

Business problem: Low-profit products are not necessarily unprofitable in every market. The analysis explores where profitability varies across products, categories, states and time.

  • Prepared the Sample Superstore dataset and developed DAX profit measures.
  • Compared category profitability and ranked the 10 least-profitable products.
  • Combined state-level profit mapping, quarterly trends from 2013–2016 and department filtering.
